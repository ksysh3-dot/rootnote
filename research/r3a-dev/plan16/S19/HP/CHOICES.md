Main metric (S19-H code reused): sub-150 absolute = mean of 5 ms boxes of the linear-phase 20-140 Hz band (dBFS); leakage-free ratio = Hann-FFT 20-140 / 150-600 (dB). 7 windows pooled (sum of energies) and per window; held-out = median over 12 songs.
Guards (19.7.1, mechanical):
  G1 onset ringing: onsets = births of the v1.6-b analysis trace = hop where a voice id enters state ON (2) from Empty/CAND (id absent or not ON in the previous hop) or REL (3) -> ON; time = hop first-output input time CA.hop_time(hop)[1] + 8/3000 s (block centre). Onset mask = union of [t, t+100 ms) over all such onsets (all voices, any frequency); energy = sum of the 5 ms boxes of the 20-140 Hz band whose box start lies in the mask; the onset set of v1.6-b is used for every row (P1 too). Pooled over the 7 windows (summed energy, dB) and held-out median. FAIL iff row > P1 AND row - v1.6-b > +1 dB (pooled 7 windows).
  G2 body 150-600 (pooled 7-window Hann FFT, dB vs v1.6-b): FAIL iff row below P1 AND row - v1.6-b < -1 dB.
  G3 V2.k2 and V2.k3 (suite, |dphi| rad): FAIL iff |row| > 0.3, or |row| > |P1| AND |row| - |v1.6-b| > 0.1.
  G4 analysis trace: all 22 fields of the full-song trace, bytes identical to v1.6-b on all 14 songs (comp 0), or all fields except psi_2 / psi_3 (18, 19) for comp 1 (psi is the compensated quantity). FAIL otherwise. This stands in for gaps / flicker / lost notes (19.7.1).
Adoption of a row: sub-150 absolute pooled 7 windows <= v1.6-b - 3 dB AND leakage-free ratio pooled <= v1.6-b - 3 dB, absolute improved in >= 5 of 7 windows, held-out median (absolute and ratio) not worse than v1.6-b, and no guard G1-G4 fails. Several pass: lowest sub-150 absolute (7 pooled); within 1 dB the smaller body (150-600) loss.
Report only: attack (suite V3a, V3h.*.rise90, V3b), 150-200 Hz change (Hann FFT, vs v1.6-b), per-window residual (20-140 dBFS gap to P1, rank), held-out body.
Excursion proxy (report only): dry = input mono mean through the app input HP, LR4 100 Hz (two cascaded Butterworth-2 at 100 Hz, causal, scipy; the engine's own filter not used) + the layer (latency removed); signal = sum, band 20-400 Hz (butter 4 sosfiltfilt). Per window / excerpt: peak |x| over the segment (dBFS), median of the 200 ms-window peaks (dBFS), and 2-12 Hz pulsing = B1 mod_series M (dB rms) of the 5 ms box RMS dB envelope (200 Hz) of the band signal, mean over the windows of the segment (2 s windows; mod_series window). Also the dry-only values for reference (layer contribution = sum - dry). Pooled over the 7 windows = max of peaks, mean of M; held-out = median over songs.
