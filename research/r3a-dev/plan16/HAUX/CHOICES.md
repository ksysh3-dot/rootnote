## 1. Data and time base
* Slakh voices = `kcommon.SongCtx(key)` (core v1.5 trace, `voices_ext`, `annotate` B2 classes, V/K cause tags, stem-aware wrong). Voice-hop i of a voice: hop h = v['hs'][i], core trace time tc[h], **input time T = tc[h] + LAG (LAG = -0.012 s, A's calibration)** = v['T'][i]; f_osc = v['f'][1:-1][i]; f_track = v['ftr'][i]; amp = v['A'][i] (> 0 required). Audio = `data.audio(key)` (48 kHz stereo, mono = (L+R)/2), the same signal the core saw.
* Hop-level class (tag at the render sample nearest to T, 3 kHz grid): **real** = B2 class matched (cls 0) and comp 0 (right layer, not wrong); **ghost** = silence class (cls 2) with comp 3..7 (stem-aware wrong; cause 1 no-bass song, 2 drum, 3 bass reverb/late, 4 other instrument, 5 other = V/K tags); **mismatch** = cls 1 with comp 2 (reported separately). Hops with amp = 0 are not used.
* Songs: Q1 per-song AUC needs both classes: the 7 bass songs (T60 T40 T01 T05 T12 T20 T25). Ghost-only songs (T23 T24 T26, no bass MIDI) are reported separately as ghost-vs-pooled-real AUC (real from the 7 bass songs; flagged as cross-song).
* Mapping check (validation, run first, reported): for matched real hops of 3 songs, correlation of the trace amplitude A c sigma (dB) with the fitted input partial energy (section 2) at input-time shifts of -30..+30 ms in 2.67 ms steps; the shift of the maximum is reported. Not used to retune T.

## 2. Auxiliary channel (48 kHz mono input, LS comb fit)
* Window: Hann (symmetric, w(n) = 0.5 (1 - cos(2 pi (n+0.5)/L))) **centred on T**, two lengths: **W40 = 1920 samples (40 ms; future 20 ms)** and **W85 = 4080 samples (85 ms; future 42.5 ms)**. Both futures are <= the 64 ms ring future (191 samples at 3 kHz) the engine holds, and well inside the look-ahead the core already uses (tracker hop reads input up to c + 118 ms, c = tc - 0.028; T = tc - 12 ms -> the core already reads ~130 ms beyond T). Past = future (centred), so "past + future" = L; the future part is what the budget concerns.
* Model (weighted LS, weights = window): columns DC + (cos, sin) at f (k = 1, **nuisance, not counted**) + (cos, sin) at k f, k = 2..Kmax, Kmax = min(12, floor(2000 / f)). Ridge 1e-6 x mean diagonal of the Gram matrix. a_k = hypot(coefficients) (peak amplitude), E_k = a_k^2 / 2.
* **Floor between partials**: residual of the harmonic fit, then a second weighted LS of (cos, sin) at (j + 1/2) f, j = 1..Kmax-1 (all on the residual, same window); E_inter,j likewise.
* **H_sal(K)** = 10 log10( mean_{k=2..K} E_k / (mean_{j=1..K-1} E_inter,j + 1e-30) ), reported for K = Kmax (H_sal12) and K = min(6, Kmax) (H_sal6). **n_hit** = number of k in 2..min(Kmax, 8) with E_k >= 4 x mean_{j} E_inter (+6 dB). **a_h(t)** = a_k, k = 2..6 (dB envelope).
* **f_h** (comb-refined f0): the zero-padded (to 65536) Hann-windowed spectrum of the same segment, amplitude |X|; score(f') = sum_{k=2..6, k f' <= 2000} |X|(k f') (linear interpolation) over f' = f_track x 2^(c/1200), c in [-100, +100] cent, 2 cent step, parabolic refinement of the maximum. f_h always = the maximum (no validity gate; a ghost without a comb simply returns a noisy f_h). LS features are computed at f_track (primary) AND at f_h (secondary, `_h` suffix).
* Reported timing: the same fits at 48 kHz are what is measured. (Decimated 8-12 kHz cost: section 6.)

## 3. Q1 existence (real vs ghost)
* Per song AUC (Mann-Whitney, ties 0.5) of **hop-level** values: H_sal12, H_sal6 (both windows, at f_track and at f_h), n_hit, and the **3 kHz K2 cue** of 16.10.2 (`kcommon.k2_at`: engine-recipe peak list, tracker f within 30 cent and a peak at 2f or 3f with rel >= r1 - 20 dB; boolean, so AUC = (TPR + TNR) / 2). Also voice-level AUC (median over the voice's hops, voices with >= 5 hops; real voices cls 0 vs ghost voices cls 2 carrying sa-wrong).
* "Best simple combination": a fixed list of candidates fixed now (no fitting): (a) H_sal12, (b) H_sal6, (c) n_hit, (d) mean of the pooled-rank percentiles of H_sal12 and n_hit, (e) H_sal6 percentile + 0.5 x K2, (f) H_sal6 at f_h. Best = highest median per-song AUC; selection among the candidates is reported as such (small optimism).
  Added before the first feature run (a synthetic self-test showed a comb at f0/2 gets H_sal ~ 45 dB because its even harmonics are the real partials): (g) **H_odd** = H_sal over odd k = 3, 5, 7, 9, 11 only (same floor, K = Kmax), which a sub-octave ghost fails; (h) **min(H_sal6, H_odd)**.
* Per-cause AUC (drum / late / other-instrument / other / no-bass) real vs ghost of that cause; all summed over bass songs pooled.
* **18.10 usefulness threshold for Q1: per-song AUC median >= 0.7** (best of the two windows, H_sal-based, including combinations).

## 4. Q2 pitch
* Population: matched voices (cls 0), hops with amp > 0 where a truth segment (`segs`, lowest bass note with `tp`) sounds at T (s <= T < e) and a multiplier m in {0.5, 1, 2, 3} puts f_track within 100 cent of m f_MIDI (the multiplier minimising |cent|). Error in cent = |1200 log2(f_x / (m f_MIDI))| for f_x in {f_osc, f_track, f_h(W40), f_h(W85)} on the SAME hops. Reported: median, p90, share > 25 cent (also share > 50 cent), pooled and per song; plus the share of matched hops excluded by the 100 cent gate.
* Hop-to-hop jitter on consecutive hops of one voice run: |delta f| in Hz, in cent, medians and p95, and the count of > 2 Hz/hop jumps per voice-second, for f_osc, f_track, f_h(W40), f_h(W85) (matched Slakh voices; and in the user windows, section 5).
* Note (stated before the run): f_h with W85 is smoother by construction (longer window = more latency in a glide); a glide check is reported (error on hops within 100 ms of a MIDI note change vs elsewhere).
* 18.10 usefulness: f_h pitch error (median or p90) smaller than f_track's, or jump rate lower than f_osc's, both reported as measured; "improvement" = f_h lower on both median and p90 error, or the jump rate of f_h at most half the f_osc one at no worse error.

## 5. User windows (Apparition, Reminiscence) and Q3
* Windows (2 s each, same as A5 `WINDOWS`): Apparition 52.0-54.0, 88.5-90.5, 123.5-125.5, 201.0-203.0, 203.0-205.0 (= 3:21-3:25), Reminiscence 96.0-98.0, 113.0-115.0.
* Trace: whole-song render through `build_core/libpvbcapi.so` (v1.5, sha 5f7f3cf7...; default16 params; `b12/api5c1.py` copy for the API), trace only (no layer, no audio saved), in HAUX/tmp, deleted at the end. Hop input time T = hop_time(h)[0] + LAG (same convention; CORE_T0 included).
* Jumps = A5's `jumps()` definition on the rendered a_2 (trace field): same slot, same voice id, state >= 2 in both hops, both a_2 > 1e-7, |20 log10(a_2(h)/a_2(h-1))| > 6 dB. For each jump: input change **D_in = |20 log10(a_in(h) / a_in(h-1))|** with a_in = fitted input partial amplitude of k = 2 from section 2, at 2 f_osc(h) at hop h and 2 f_osc(h-1) at hop h-1 (follows the voice), both windows. **Unsupported jump = D_in < 2 dB.** Variant (fixed frequency 2 f_osc(h) in both hops) and the 3-hop variant (D_in over h-2 -> h+1, i.e. 16 ms) are reported next to it. Controls: the distribution of D_in over ALL checked hop pairs (non-jump pairs), and over the pairs with a large input envelope change, to show D_in can exceed 2 dB at all.
* f_h vs f_osc in these windows: the count of > 2 Hz/hop jumps (same slot/voice, consecutive hops, state >= 2) of f_h (W40, W85; comb refinement around f_track, then the voice's own f_osc-based LS is not used) vs of f_osc, per window and per voice-second; for jumps of f_osc > 2 Hz, the share where f_h moved by less than 1 Hz.
* 18.10 usefulness for Q3: "unsupported" share >= 30 %.
