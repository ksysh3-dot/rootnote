# Review of 16.13 (bass-context gate), read-only, Sonnet

Facts checked: 16.9 per-song silence numbers (v_report.txt): T26 0.0516, T24 0.0146, T23 0.0005 (no-bass); T25 0.1155 of 0.2121 (54 %); bass songs T01 0.0127, T05 0.0072, T12 0.0024, T20 0.0069, T60 0.0007, T40 0. Ghost voices that carry silence-wrong energy live a median 0.39-0.44 s (p90 0.7-1.3 s, p99 4-7 s) with 141-145 such voices per 30 s in T24/T26 (v_raw.json vstat). conf_list.json was built with the rule 'Bass-stem MIDI union >= 10 s in [0,30] s'.

## BLOCKING

B1. P is a union over all voices and time, but 0c tests a per-voice-hop rate. Why it matters: in a no-bass song there are ~5 ghost voices/s with ~0.4 s lifetimes. If even 3 % of them pass 'ON, f range <= 30 cent over 100-150 ms, amp >= -30 dB', a stable event occurs every ~7 s, so P=1 for almost all of the time at H = 2, 4 s (and often at H = 1). The (A) <= 15 % hop test says nothing about this. The gate would then never close in T24/T26 (the main target) and the main metric fails after the whole grid is spent. K1 at 20 ms already had 57 % true in A. Also the gate is self-confirming: a ghost that becomes stable opens the gate for itself and all others.
Replacement for 0c: 'Run the actual gate (g_cap = -24 dB, every H, D_s) on the core v1.5 trace. Report per song (T24, T26, T23, and T25 separately) (i) fraction of time with P=0, (ii) fraction of silence-wrong energy emitted while P=0 (R_P), (iii) stable-voice events per second in the 'no bass sounding' periods, (iv) the same fractions for bass-song hops where a stem-aware bass note is sounding (P must be 1 for >= 98 % of those hops). Stop if for every (H, D_s) R_P over {T24, T26} < 50 % or (iv) < 98 %. The per-hop (A)/(B) rates are reported only.'

B2. 'Amp percentile', 'ON / coasting' and start-up are undefined, and an offline array implementation would leak the future. 'amp 95 percentile (past cumulative)' does not say over which population (all slots incl. zero? ON hops only? per voice?), what the warm-up is, or that it must be a running statistic. np.percentile over the whole song would silently use the future. Also 'ON を維持 (coasting 제외)' does not say what an ON->REL->ON blip or a voice-id reuse does to the D_s window, nor which trace field is 'f (필드 5)' (index is 0-based f_track; coasting is column 4, state==2 is ON).
Replacement: 'Per hop h, using only trace rows with hop <= h: a voice (trace column oice) is stable at h iff state==2 and coasting==0 at all of hops h-N_s+1..h (N_s = ceil(D_s/5.333 ms): 19 for 100 ms, 29 for 150 ms), max-min of 1200*log2(f_track) over that window <= 30, and amp = A*c*sigma >= q95(h-1) - 30 dB. q95(h-1) is the running 95th percentile of amp over all (voice, hop) rows with state==2, coasting==0 and hop < h, kept as a 1 dB-bin histogram updated after each hop. While fewer than 400 such rows exist the amp condition is true. A voice-id change, REL or CAND state resets its window. P(h)=1 iff some voice is stable at any hop in (h-H/5.333ms, h]. The offline code logs, for every decision, the max trace hop index read; it must be <= h.'

B3. 'First note after rest' set uses the wrong rest definition, and song/clip start is uncovered. The gate closes only after H s (1/2/4) with no stable voice, but the metric and oracle (0b) use 'bass silence >= 1 s'. For H=2,4 many notes in the set are never gated (diluting the cost), and for the 0b oracle the -24 dB ceiling is computed for a gate that cannot exist (it closes after >= H s, not 1 s). The first note of each clip (P=0 until t=H by definition) belongs to neither the rise90 (excluded) nor the first-note metric (no 'previous silence'). Dev clips are 30 s excerpts, so 'song start' is an arbitrary cut, yet every setting gets credit for muting ghosts in [0,H).
Replacement: 'The first-note set for a setting is every stem-aware bass note whose onset hop has P=0 under that setting (this includes notes in [0, H)). 0b is computed per H with the oracle silence threshold equal to H (not 1 s). The primary metric and F exclude t < 4 s for all settings (so every cell is compared on the same span); the unrestricted values are reported.'

## MAJOR

M1. The -3 dB per-song / <= 25 % of notes below -6 dB first-note thresholds are almost unreachable by construction. A new voice is CAND then ON and needs D_s of stable pitch; with g_cap -12 dB for 150 ms of the first 200 ms the energy is (150*0.063+50)/200 = 0.30 = -5.3 dB (100 ms: -2.8 dB at best, before the CAND delay and attack f jitter). Ghost-free songs fail this by design, and 'redefine once after 0d' lets the threshold be set from the observed result (pure post-hoc).
Replacement: 'First-note thresholds are fixed now: per song first 200 ms right-layer energy >= core - 5 dB, and <= 35 % of notes drop by more than 6 dB. 0d may only report; if the best cell at its g_cap violates either, that cell is dropped. No post-hoc redefinition.'

M2. Main-metric arithmetic: no-bass songs are 0.0667/0.2121 = 31.6 % of the target, and a -12 dB cap removes at most 94 % of it (29.6 %). The 35 % sum can therefore only be met with g_cap=-24 dB plus >= 3-5 % from bass songs, and T25 (54 %) alone can decide it. The per-song 'majority of 7 songs with core >= 0.002' (T24, T26, T25, T01, T05, T12, T20 = need 4) lets T24+T26+T25 plus one bass song pass, while the bass-song half (the 'rests and song start' part of the goal) is never required.
Replacement: 'Pass needs all of: sum excluding T25 reduced >= 45 %; T25 reported and its reduction must not be negative; no-bass songs T24 and T26 each >= 50 % and T23 core+0.0005; at least 2 of {T01, T05, T12, T20} reduced >= 25 % (or 'not testable' if their R_long (0a) is < 10 %, in which case the claim is limited to no-bass songs).'

M3. Confirmation songs cannot test the main design. conf_list.json requires >= 10 s of bass MIDI in the first 30 s (values 18.7-27.1 s), so there are zero no-bass songs and little rest time; the primary-reference rule ('baseline >= 0.002 for >= 2 songs else not testable') is likely to land in 'not testable', and the decision table has no row for that outcome (16.10 had one too and it only changed text). Result: the gate would be adopted on dev alone or deadlocked.
Replacement: 'Before running, build conf2 from slakh2100/train only by a fixed rule: the 5 songs of conf_list.json plus the first 3 non-excluded train songs (ascending number) with NO Bass-stem MIDI in [0,30] s and the first 2 with Bass-stem MIDI union in [3,10] s (rests). Record paths/hashes in CHOICES. Decision: 'not testable' for the primary metric means adoption is withheld and the C++ pre-registration is not written; only no-bass-song claims need the new no-bass conf songs.' (check that train contains such songs; do not touch validation/test).

M4. Missing harm guards. 16.13 lists the 16.10 guard names only. Not covered: (a) 16.10.1 M4 additions 'note loss' (후반 2/3 -6 dB) and 'sustained pumping' - exactly what the P 1->0 fade does to a long note whose pitch is never 'stable'; (b) bass with vibrato/slide/portamento/808 pitch drops, where f moves > 30 cent per 100 ms and no voice is ever stable so P=0 and real bass is muted; (c) quiet bass after a loud passage (>30 dB below the running p95); (d) sparse bass (isolated notes 1.5-4 s apart, all attacks cut by g_cap); (e) pumping at P transitions in songs where P toggles with period H.
Replacement: 'Guards add: 16.10.1 M4 (note loss, sustained-note pumping on all notes, not only detector overlap); vib_100_5/6 and glide from 16.8.1 M6: no more than core + 0.5 dB/note-level change and P stays 1 within 300 ms of the first note; synthetic sparse bass (0.3 s notes every 2.5 s): first-note rise90 <= core + 40 ms and note energy >= core - 5 dB; quiet passage (note at -35 dB after a 5 s loud passage): not muted; real-song count of P 1->0 transitions that occur while a stem-aware bass note sounds must be 0 (report their number).' All guard signals are measured after the first stable voice (otherwise every short signal is dominated by the P=0 start).

M5. Oracle (0b) reaches only the gate's maximum, not its decision quality. 'R_max < 50 % stop' should use the real P-based R_P (B1) not stem-silence, and 0a R_long (<40 % stop) is dominated by the no-bass songs (31.6 % alone) so it passes even if nothing in bass songs is reachable. Report 0a per song and R_long for bass songs separately; stop if R_long (bass songs excluding T25) < 10 % and T25's R_long < 20 %.

## MINOR

m1. No-bass songs: F ('right layer') is 0/0 per song; state 'songs with zero right energy are excluded from F, and their F is judged only through the sum'. The gate also mutes real low-pitched content of other instruments (piano/synth left hand, cello); cause 1 labels all of it 'wrong' regardless of MIDI. Add report-only: layer energy matching other-instrument MIDI pitch (0.5/1/2/3x, 50 cent) before/after, per no-bass song. Hearing impact is a user matter (no listening in this chain), but the number should be printed.
m2. Gaming: g_cap fixed at the lower end and a never-stable setting would be an 'always -24 dB' gate; F >= core - 0.5 dB blocks it in bass songs only. Add: 'P=0 time fraction in bass songs (while a stem-aware bass note sounds) <= 2 %' (included in B1(iv)).
m3. Specify P(t) update: 'P is computed from hops <= h and applied to hop h's amp; the 10 ms ramp and 30 ms fade start at hop h (no look-ahead).' Also state the gain in dB per hop, and that a voice born at P=0 keeps g_cap until its own stable event or a stable event of another voice.
m4. Report grid-wide the total count of P transitions per minute (pumping proxy), and T40/T60 values (core ~0): use 'core + 0.0005' for them as already written.
