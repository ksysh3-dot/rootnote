# "저음 맞추기" (bass fit): in-app listening tests

Status: 2026-10-24. Engine knob (step 0), the pure test logic (`pvb/BassFit.kt`, `pvb/BassFitSession.kt`; host tests
`jvm/BassFitTest.kt`, `jvm/BassFitSessionTest.kt`) and the screen (`devmode/BassFitActivity.kt`, `BassFitScreen.kt`,
opened from the top of developer mode) done; not yet tried on a phone. First shipped in developer mode only; opened to
everyone after the S26 Ultra result matches the UMIK-1 measurement.

## Goal

Let the user find, by ear and blind, the settings their own phone needs: where the speaker stops (floor), how much
virtual bass, where harmonics break up, and (optionally) the harmonic shape. No third-party data needed.

## Common rules

- **Volume fixed** at the user's usual listening volume, recorded with the result; a volume change pauses the test.
  The volume link scales the result for other volumes.
- **Signals generated by the app** (no copyrighted audio). The final check uses the user's own playing music.
- **Blind:** A/B/X order random every trial; catch trials (silence, layer off) mixed in, at least two per staircase.
- **A new order every run:** each run draws a fresh seed (SecureRandom); every part takes its own seed from it, so the
  catch trials' places, the pairs' order and the A/B/X sides differ each time. The seed is shown with the result
  ("시험 코드") and kept with a saved fit, so a run can be replayed exactly.
- **ABX decision:** up to 8 trials; "distinguishable" at 7/8 or better (one-sided binomial p < 0.05); stop early at 6/6.
- **Consistency:** repeated pairs; under 70 % agreement, the step's result is not applied (defaults kept).
- **"No difference" keeps the lower / default value.** Safety caps always apply: low-mid boost <= +3 dB, virtual bass
  <= break-up limit - 3 dB, test signals below the S26 Ultra's measured protection threshold.
- Results are saved as "my fit", separate from the defaults, reversible, and included in the settings share code.

## Noise gate (strict)

Before step 1, with the phone microphone (the permission the measurement features already use):

1. Record 3 s of the room with nothing playing.
2. Play the test reference (the test band at test level) for 2 s and record it.
3. Per third-octave band of the test (63-500 Hz): the margin, and how much the room level jumps (100 ms frames:
   max - median). In the bands the speaker plays (within 20 dB of its strongest test band) the margin is the
   signal-to-noise ratio. A phone speaker barely plays 63-100 Hz, so the ratio there would fail in any room; those
   bands are judged by the room against the speaker's strongest band instead (a rumble there still masks the tones
   above it, and fails).
4. **Pass only if** every margin >= 35 dB and the room level jumps by <= 6 dB. Everything is a ratio through one
   microphone (UNPROCESSED where offered, else VOICE_RECOGNITION), so an uncalibrated phone microphone is enough. The
   same recording gives the latency (where the reference starts), used to find each trial's silent interval.
5. Otherwise: "주변이 시끄러워서 결과가 부정확할 수 있어요! 그래도 진행할까요?" [조용한 곳에서 다시] [그래도 진행].
   Proceeding marks the result "noisy" (lower confidence; never used as shared data).
6. No microphone permission: "주변이 조용한지 확인할 수 없어요" with the same choice.
7. During step 1 the silent interval of each two-interval trial is monitored; a noise burst 6 dB over the room in a
   test band discards that trial and repeats it (three times at most, then it is answered).

## Steps

| Step | What it finds | Method | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Speaker floor (lowest note the speaker itself plays cleanly) | Two-interval forced choice ("어느 쪽에서 소리가 났나요?"), tone bursts from 300 Hz down in 1/6-octave steps, 2-down/1-up staircase (Levitt 1971; QUEST/Psi possible later), option "윙·지직만 났어요" counts as not heard; optional phone-mic bass check alongside. Then ABX: edge at floor vs floor x 1.3 on a 150-250 Hz bass line; no difference keeps the lower | 3 min |
| 2 | Is the layer audible; preferred strength | ABX off vs +4; then blind A/B "better / no difference" climbing over 0..+8 dB in 2 dB steps from the middle (2 vs 4, then 0 vs 2 or 4 vs 6, then 6 vs 8); ties take the lower; the first pair is asked again for consistency | 2 min |
| 3 | Break-up limit | Yes/no "저음이 지직거리거나 찌그러지나요?" on the bass clip, layer 0..12 dB in 2 dB steps, 1-up/1-down from the preferred strength (converges where break-up is heard half the time); catch trials with the layer off (a "buzz" there = false alarm; over a third = unreliable). Break-up = mean of the last four reversals; limit = break-up - 3 dB, whole dB. Clean three times in a row at 12 dB and never buzzing there: no limit. (Not an ABX of a level against 6 dB lower: any audible layer differs from itself by 6 dB, so that ABX only finds the levels differ.) | 2 min |
| 4 (optional, precision) | Harmonic shape | Two knobs only: tilt (existing roll-off value) and low-order weight (H2 vs H3-5, existing harmonic offsets). 3 x 3 grid, ~18-20 blind pairs chosen where the ranking is uncertain, Bradley-Terry scores; Gaussian-process preference learning later if needed | 5-7 min |
| Check | New vs previous settings on real music | Live blind A/B (existing) | 1 min |

## What each result sets

| Result | Settings |
|---|---|
| Speaker floor F | high-pass = max(80, F / 1.5) (100 Hz at the S26 Ultra's 150); generator edge = 190 x F/150, at most 280 (1.3 times that when the edge check told them apart and the higher was preferred twice); output band low edge = 150 x F/150; "배음 표 기준 바닥" = F (developer value `table_floor`, 115-260 Hz; engine `P_D_TABLE_SCALE` = F/150). Developer mode draws F as the speaker floor in its graphs. Unreliable floor: none of these change |
| Preferred strength | virtual bass strength (capped by step 3) |
| Break-up limit | strength cap (the volume link is held off during the test and left as it is) |
| Harmonic shape | roll-off offset, harmonic offsets k = 2..5 |

## Engine (step 0, done)

`P_D_TABLE_SCALE` (index 56; 1 or 0 = model, 0.75..1.75): the virtual-bass weight/gain table is looked up at
f / scale, and the table's upper frequency clamp moves with it. The generator edge may now go up to 280 Hz. Default
and zero are bit-identical to the model (test_devmode "in tree"); a moved value changes the output.

First host measurement (layer level re the source band, dB; model = S26 Ultra):

| Note | 55 | 98 | 131 | 147 | 165 | 185 | 200 | 220 | 240 Hz |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Model | -17 | -9 | -13 | -23 | -33 | -34 | none | none | none |
| Floor 200 (scale 1.33, edge 253, out low 200) | -24 | -23 | -16 | -18 | -21 | -27 | -35 | -44 | -45 |

The scale fills 147-240 Hz as intended but also lowers 55-98 Hz notes, because the table's level matching is tied to
the S26 Ultra's speaker. Before the scale becomes a default, check it with a frequency-shifted speaker model
(`study/phone_model.py`): the perceived bass on a "floor 200" speaker with the scale should match the S26 Ultra's with
the model.

## Screen (developer mode)

Intro (volume, quiet room, nothing changes before saving; the last saved fit and "맞추기 전으로 되돌리기") -> room
check -> questions, answers in a fixed bottom bar, buttons enabled once the clips were heard (X for an ABX, both A and
B for a pair, the clip for a buzz question, the whole trial for step 1) -> result (what was found, the values that
change) -> the listener's own music, three blind pairs of the current and the new settings (optional) -> save.

- A volume change, another output or the engine stopping pauses (the sound stops) and repeats the question when back.
- Clips restart from their beginning with the side's settings (the engine takes them 200 ms before the clip).
- Saving writes the developer values (and developer mode on) and keeps the values before it, the record line and the
  floor (pvb_flags); "맞추기 전으로 되돌리기" writes them back. Leaving the screen stores nothing; leaving during the
  questions starts over, a finished run (result, music check, save) waits while another app is open.
- The screen stays on during a run. A step-1 trial disturbed three times in a row is answered anyway (a steady noise
  must not stall the test). The microphone runs only during the room check and step 1.

## Open decisions

- First release place: developer mode (now) or advanced settings.
- Optional phone-mic bass check in step 1.
- Strength pairs are not matched in loudness (a louder layer tends to win; ties keep the lower level).
- First phone run: S26 Ultra, step 1 against the UMIK-1 floor (150 Hz); the tone level (-15 dBFS) may need moving.
- Outside developer mode the results must apply without `P_DEV_ON` (the D_* values are developer-only today).

## Test logic check (simulated listeners, BassFitTest)

- Guessing listener judged "distinguished" in 3.6 % of ABX runs; a 95 %-right listener in 94 %.
- Floor staircase: median error 0.08-0.09 octave, 90th percentile 0.25-0.29 octave (true floors 130-240 Hz,
  logistic listener); a listener who "hears" silent catch trials is marked unreliable.
- Room check: a quiet room passes, a TV-like room and a voice burst fail; a speaker silent below 125 Hz in a quiet
  room passes; a 63 Hz rumble 28 dB under the strongest band fails. Latency found within 10 ms.
- Strength: lands on the level nearest the listener's ideal; ties keep the lower; a reversed repeat is flagged.
- Break-up (yes/no): median error 0.5 dB, 90th percentile 1-1.5 dB (break-up at 5-11.5 dB); limits at least 1 dB
  under the break-up in 193-200 of 200 runs; "buzz" on catch trials -> unreliable in 100 of 100.
- Session: whole runs with simulated listeners land on the floor, preferred strength, cap and shape they were given;
  the same seed replays the same questions, another seed changes the order and the sides.
- Shape grid: the preferred cell found in 37-40 of 40 runs with 18 pairs (noisy listener).
