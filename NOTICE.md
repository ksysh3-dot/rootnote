# NOTICE

## This is a modified version (fork) of RootlessJamesDSP

This repository contains **Rootnote** (general distribution build, package `kr.ksysh3.rootnote`) and **PVB**
(personal Galaxy S26 Ultra build, package `kr.ksysh3.pvb.s26u`). Both are an **unofficial fork** of
[RootlessJamesDSP](https://github.com/ThePBone/RootlessJamesDSP) **1.6.14** (upstream commit `60d25ae`), modified by
ksysh3 from **2026-10-16** onwards. They are not made, endorsed or supported by the RootlessJamesDSP or JamesDSP
developers. Please do not report problems of this fork to the upstream projects.

What the fork changes, in short: a new audio engine for phone speakers (speaker correction EQ, true-peak limiter,
virtual bass, loudness matching) in `app/src/main/cpp/libpvb` and `app/src/main/java/.../pvb`, which processes the
captured audio instead of the JamesDSP engine while it is on; its settings screen and status card; and, in the general
build, device profiles, safe limits, a first-run notice and the removal of research features that store records.
